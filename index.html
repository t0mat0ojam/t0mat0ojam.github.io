Vision

I'm a high schooler on a mission: to push the boundaries of research and transform discoveries into real-world impact for communities around me.

My journey begins at the intersection of curiosity and action — exploring scientific questions that matter, then building ventures that carry those answers from the lab into everyday life.

I believe the next generation of entrepreneurs won't just build companies. They'll be researcher-founders who see problems others miss and have the tools to solve them from first principles.

My goal is simple: conduct meaningful research, then give it back — to my community, to society, and to the world.
