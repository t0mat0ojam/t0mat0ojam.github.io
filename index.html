Researching compression methods for biosignal decoders and building low-cost embedded systems. I want assistive and rehab devices to reach people recovering from strokes and other neurological injuries. Also interested in AI safety.

Researcher @ National Institute of Informatics and the University of Tokyo's Matsuo–Iwasawa Lab.

What I'm building

  • Low-cost EMG controllerPrototyping

    Reads muscle signals from surface electrodes and turns them into commands for assistive devices such as exoskeletons. Designed around parts a clinic or a student can afford.

    Applying for grant funding

  • prunecalUnder review

    Open-source Python package that combines structured pruning with label-free batch-norm recalibration. Deployed so EEG decoders and other bio-signal decoders can run on low cost devices.

    With Assoc. Prof. Mahito Sugiyama, National Institute of Informatics. Funded by JST's STELLA program.
